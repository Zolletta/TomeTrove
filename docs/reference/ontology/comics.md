# Comics

Narrative or content expressed through sequential art and images (comics, manga, graphic novels, bande dessinée, picture books).

## Genre hierarchies

| Classification           | Description                                | Examples                                                                 |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `Comics/Adventure`       | Adventure comics                           | *Tintin* (Hergé), *Lone Wolf and Cub* (Kazuo Koike)                      |
| `Comics/Children`        | Picture books and children's comics        | *Where the Wild Things Are* (Maurice Sendak), *Dog Man* (Dav Pilkey)     |
| `Comics/Experimentation` | Experimental comics and visual avant-garde | *A Contract with God* (Will Eisner), *Here* (Richard McGuire)            |
| `Comics/Fantasy`         | Fantasy comics and manga                   | *The Sandman* (Neil Gaiman), *Fullmetal Alchemist* (Hiromu Arakawa)      |
| `Comics/History`         | Comics about historical events             | *Barefoot Gen* (Keiji Nakazawa)                                          |
| `Comics/Horror`          | Horror comics and manga                    | *Uzumaki* (Junji Ito), *Swamp Thing* (Alan Moore)                        |
| `Comics/Humor`           | Humorous comics                            | *Calvin and Hobbes* (Bill Watterson), *Asterix* (Goscinny & Uderzo)      |
| `Comics/Mystery`         | Graphic novel mystery                      | *Blacksad* (Juan Díaz Canales), *Batman: The Long Halloween* (Jeph Loeb) |
| `Comics/Romance`         | Romance comics and manga                   | *Fruits Basket* (Natsuki Takaya), *Blankets* (Craig Thompson)            |
| `Comics/Science Fiction` | Sci-fi comics and manga                    | *Akira* (Katsuhiro Otomo), *Saga* (Brian K. Vaughan)                     |
